1. Sign in and get oriented

  1. Sign in with the email and password registered for your approved account.
  2. If enabled, enter the current six-digit code from your authenticator app.
  3. Use the navigation menu to open Dashboard, Orders, Products, Inventory, Integrations, My profile, and Help & support.
  4. Sign out from the account menu when using a shared computer.

2. Configure companies and integrations

  1. Open Companies and add the business/company record used by your sales channel.
  2. Open Company Integrations, choose the company and channel, then enter the credentials requested by the form.
  3. Save the integration and confirm its status is connected before syncing data.
  4. Keep API keys and passwords private. If a credential is exposed, rotate it in the relevant channel.
  5. For shipping, configure the available carrier integration and its services before dispatching orders.

Only grant UniSell the permissions required for the features you use. Integration names and available fields depend on the account configuration.

3. Add and manage products

  1. Open Products and choose Add product to create a listing.
  2. Fill in the required product details such as title, SKU, price, description, images, and variants as applicable.
  3. Save the product. Use the product list to search and review saved listings.
  4. Double-click a product row to open its edit screen.
  5. Select product checkboxes to reveal bulk edit or delete actions. Review the selected count before applying a bulk action.
  6. Use the channel push/sync options when you want to publish supported products to connected channels.

4. Manage inventory

  1. Open Inventory to review quantities and allocated stock.
  2. Double-click a row to edit the product's inventory quantity.
  3. Select one or more rows to use bulk editing or deletion where available.
  4. Check the displayed quantity after saving. Connected channel stock synchronization depends on the integration and its permissions.
  5. Review dashboard stock alerts for low or out-of-stock items.

5. Review and process orders

  1. After connecting a sales channel, open Orders and sync or refresh imported orders.
  2. Open an order to check its customer, line items, address, payment, and fulfillment status.
  3. To create a shipment, use the consignment workflow and select a configured carrier and service.
  4. Dispatch when the shipment details are correct. Use the tracking number and label links to follow up.
  5. If an order or shipment fails, note its order number and the error shown before submitting a support request.

6. Account and security

  1. Open My profile to update your display name, avatar, or email. Email changes take effect only after confirming the new address.
  2. To change your password, enter your current password and choose a unique password of at least 12 characters.
  3. Enable authenticator-based two-factor authentication from the security section of your profile.
  4. Review active sessions and sign out other devices you no longer use.
  5. Use Help & support if you cannot access your account.

7. Troubleshooting and support

  • Data did not sync: Confirm the integration is connected, credentials are current, and the account has the necessary channel permissions.
  • Product was not imported: Check the connected company/channel and product visibility in the source channel, then retry a sync.
  • Stock looks incorrect: Check the product variant/SKU and allocation before changing the quantity.
  • Shipment failed: Verify the order address and carrier service configuration, then include the order number and error in a support ticket.
  • Need help? Open Help & support, search the quick answers, or submit a ticket. Include steps to reproduce the issue and a screenshot when useful. Never attach passwords, API secrets, payment-card data, or other sensitive customer information.
  • After submitting a ticket, save its ticket number and follow its status or send another message from My tickets. Email copies and updates are sent when mail delivery is configured.
  • Support staff with a Superadmin account can use the Support inbox on this page to respond to tickets and update their status.

8. Good practices

  • Check product data and inventory before pushing updates to sales channels.
  • Use unique account passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep integration credentials secure and rotate them if exposed.
  • Review order and stock alerts regularly.
  • Sign out on shared devices and remove sessions you do not recognize.
